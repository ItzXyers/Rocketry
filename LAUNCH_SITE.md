# Launch Site — Gothenburg region

**Question:** find a suitable launch site near Gothenburg for the project's vehicles.
**Doc date:** 2026‑09‑07 · **Scope:** low/mid‑power model rockets, all current airframes
(`PhoenixABS40‑43`, `SampleRocketABS33‑36`, `SampleRocketABS31‑33`).

> This is a research note, not a booking. Every regulatory figure below is sourced (see
> §7); re‑confirm the live values before a launch day, especially the fire ban (§4) and
> the airport agreement (§3).

---

## 0. Bottom line

**Join [Svenska Modellraketklubben (SMRK)](https://smrk.space) and fly at `RT flygfält,
Högsäter`** (Färgelanda, Dalsland). It is the nearest site to Gothenburg that is already
legal, insured and set up for exactly this class of rocket:

| | |
|---|---|
| Distance from Gothenburg | ≈ **110 km** north / ≈ 1 h 20–30 min by car (E45 through Dalsland) |
| Class | SMRK's established **west‑coast low/medium‑power** site — Dalsland's only airfield, a model‑flying field run by a local group |
| 2026 launch days already scheduled | **Jan 31, Feb 21, Apr 18, May 16, Jun 6** (check `smrk.space/kalender` for the current list — west‑coast dates are concentrated in H1) |
| Regulatory load on you | Essentially zero — the club holds the field agreement and the insurance path; you turn up as a member |

A search out to **~220 km** (double the Högsäter distance) found **no other club or
insured site** — SMRK's remaining fields are by Stockholm (~450 km). Everything the wider
radius adds is **DIY farmland you arrange yourself** (§5): closest good belts are
**Vårgårda–Herrljunga–Vara** (~65–110 km E) and the **Varberg–Falkenberg** coastal plain
(~65–110 km S); **Falbygden** (~110–140 km) is the large‑field reserve. §6 is the spec
for picking an actual field in one of those belts. **§5.0** answers "can we just use
public land?" — short version: not cleanly, and a disused gravel pit or a phoned grass
airfield beats it.

Use Högsäter (or a club launch) for the **Phoenix first flights** — and note `DESIGN.md`
§4.1/§7.9 say Phoenix is **not flightworthy yet** (stability unconfirmed on rev 5, mass
model stale, 7/7 recovery tests open). Site the shakedown for **A8/B4**, not C.

## 2. Swedish rules — the "no permit" regime

Reference: **TSFS 2013:77** (*obemannade raketer och uppsläpp av föremål för rörelse i
luften*), plus SMRK's own summary of the legal stack.

| Rule | For our rockets |
|---|---|
| **Transportstyrelsen permit** | Required only for rockets **> 500 g**, **> 100 g P1 propellant**, or containing metal. Apply **30 days** ahead; ≈ 1 700 kr per handling‑hour (2026). → **Not required** for any current vehicle. Stay under 500 g and this stays true. |
| **Airport proximity** (§3) | Within **10 km straight‑line** of an airport with ATC, launch **only after agreement with that airport**. → **Binding near Gothenburg.** Högsäter is clear of it. |
| **Ordningslagen 3 kap. 7 §** | Firing rockets / pyrotechnics **on public land** generally needs a **police permit** (*tillstånd*). → Avoided by launching on **private land with the owner's consent**. |
| **LBE — lag (2010:1011) om brandfarliga och explosiva varor** (MSB) | Motor **storage/handling** permit threshold raised (April 2025) to **2 000 g propellant**; electric ignition can also trigger it. → A hobby motor stock is far under 2 000 g, so **no LBE permit** for these quantities. Transport and store the motors sensibly regardless. |
| **Eldningsförbud** (municipal fire ban) | Not a rocketry rule but overrides everything (§4). |
| **Landowner permission** | Always required, public or private. |
| **Insurance** | Not legally mandated, but third‑party liability cover comes with **SMFF / Tripoli** membership via SMRK — the practical reason to fly with the club rather than solo. |

**Net:** for these vehicles the only hard external constraint near Gothenburg is the
**10 km airport‑agreement rule** plus **landowner + fire ban**. No altitude permit, no
explosives permit.

## 3. Airspace and airports around Gothenburg

| Element | Where | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Göteborg Landvetter (ESGG)** | ~25–30 km ESE of the city | ATC airport → **10 km agreement radius**. CTR (Class C) when tower active; Göteborg TMA (Class C) above. Commercial traffic — an ad‑hoc rocket window is unrealistic here. |
| **Säve / Göteborg City (ESGP)** | Hisingen, NW of the city | Closed to scheduled airliners since 2015 but **active** for business jets, GA, helicopter and military → its own **10 km radius** covering northern Gothenburg / Hisingen. |
| **Göteborg TMA** | Overlies the whole region, Class C | Not a permit issue for < 500 g rockets, but it is why you keep close‑in flights low and why the 10 km rule is the operative limit. |

Between the two 10 km circles, the Göteborg TMA, and the terrain (§5), the metro area is
effectively closed to casual rocketry. Anything within ~15 km of the city needs a phone
call to Landvetter and/or Säve **and** a wind‑direction plan that keeps the flight away
from their approach paths — not worth it when Högsäter exists.

## 4. Fire ban (eldningsförbud)

- **Check the municipality's current status the morning of every launch** — early September
  can still carry a ban after a dry summer. Sources: the kommun's website, the county
  administrative board (Länsstyrelsen Västra Götaland), or the `brandrisk.se` / MSB app.
- Even with no ban: launch from **bare soil, gravel or a steel blast plate**, never dry
  grass; keep **water and an extinguisher** at the pad; no recovery of a still‑hot motor
  into stubble.
- A ban is absolute — it does not matter that the motor is "only" an A8.

## 5. The wider search — options out to ~220 km

The land within ~40 km of Gothenburg is **forest, exposed granite, lake and archipelago**,
cut into **small fragmented fields**: a rocket that drifts off lands in **spruce**
(unrecoverable) or **water**, and most open ground is inside the Landvetter or Säve 10 km
circle. So the search was pushed out to roughly **double the Högsäter distance (~220 km)**.

### 5.0 "Can we just launch from flat public ground?"

**Direct answer: there is no public open ground near Gothenburg that beats asking a
landowner, and the map looks more promising than it is.** The idea behind the question —
that *ordningslagen 3:7* only bites when people or property are nearby — is right as far
as it goes, but 3:7 is not the binding constraint, and on the genuinely‑public open land
around Gothenburg the rules are **tighter** than on a stubble field where one farmer's
"yes" settles it.

| Gate | On a remote public spot |
|---|---|
| **Ordningslagen 3 kap. 7 §** — police permit for pyrotechnics | Turns on *risk to person/property given time and place*. Remote, nobody around, no structures → weak constraint. **This part of the premise holds.** |
| **Kommun ordningsföreskrifter** | Almost every kommun adds its **own** fireworks/pyrotechnics rules — often a blanket permit requirement or set-dates-only. **Overrides the "no risk" logic.** Check per kommun — it's a phone call to the kommun, not something to reason out. |
| **Nature‑reserve / national‑park föreskrifter** | The flat open public land on the map — coastal heaths, mires — is largely **protected**. Reserves routinely ban open fire except at anvisade platser, ban **markslitage** activities without permit, ban terrain driving (Fjärås Bräcka does all three). **Worse than a farmer's field.** |
| **Allemansrätten** | Covers *being* on the land. Grants **no right to light a fire** or create ignition risk; full liability if it spreads. Guidance: **gravel/sand base**, never peat/moss/soil. A motor is an ignition source in that bracket. |
| **TSFS 2013:77 — 10 km airport rule** · **LBE (motors)** | **Unchanged by land status.** Apply on the remotest bog exactly as anywhere. (Both are satisfied for these rockets — §2 — but they don't get *easier* by going public.) |

**Net:** a remote spot dodges the *police* permit, but the **kommun föreskrifter**,
**reserve rules**, and **fire‑spread liability** remain — and on genuinely‑public open
land (reserves, bogs, clearcuts) the last two usually bite harder than a landowner ask.

#### 5.0.1 Public‑ish, low‑friction options — ranked

1. **A disused gravel / sand pit (grustäkt).** Bare mineral floor = the fire‑safe base the
   official guidance actually points to; flat, open, usually idle for years. It **has an
   owner** (aggregate company or estate) so it is an ask — but low‑stakes, and a "no"
   costs nothing. Find them: OSM `landuse=quarry`, or SGU / Länsstyrelsen täkt registers;
   then check satellite for an idle, dry, flat floor with a vehicle track in. Clusters
   mapped inland of Kungsbacka and around Alingsås–Vårgårda. **Best "found something".**
2. **A quiet grass airfield, by arrangement.** Not public ground — an *active* field where
   you phone the club for a slot — but flat, 500–800 m, run by people who understand
   airspace (the Högsäter pattern, closer, minus the rocket club):
   - **Rödene flygfält**, ~4 km NE of Alingsås (≈ 57.95 N, 12.58 E) — ~55 km from
     Gothenburg. Via Alingsås Modellflygklubb (`info@amfk.se`) or the local flying club.
   - **Backamo flygfält (ESGA)**, S of Uddevalla (≈ 58.18 N, 11.97 E) — ~55 km. Backamo
     Flygklubb (UL). The surrounding **old military övningsfält** is open heath with
     allemansrätt access — worth a look, but confirm it is not still in use and check
     fire rules (status unverified).
   - **Vårgårda flygfält** (≈ 58.04 N, 12.79 E) — ~75 km.
3. **A large clearcut (hygge)** on Sveaskog / kommun forest. Allemansrätt applies, it's
   open — but stumps and slash wreck recovery and dry brash is a fire load. Damp day,
   A–B motors only.

**Not worth pursuing:** coastal heath **reserves** (Sandsjöbacka, Fjärås Bräcka,
Kungsbackafjorden — fire‑restricted, sensitive, visited); **mires/bogs** (wet = lost
rocket, peat = worst fire base, often protected); **beaches** (people, often reserve, sea
breeze carries the rocket to water).

> Name a specific spot and it can be checked against the reserve register, the airport
> 10 km circles, and terrain.

### 5.1 Headline finding

**No club, insured, or established rocketry site exists in the doubled radius other than
Högsäter.** SMRK is the only model‑rocket club in Sweden; its other sites (Tullinge,
Långtora) are by Stockholm at ~450 km — outside even the doubled radius. Everything the
wider search adds is **DIY farmland** — better or worse open ground you would have to
arrange yourself — plus one historical military range that is not bookable (§5.4).

Because these vehicles need **no permit** (§2), farmland at 200 km is not "more legal"
than farmland at 60 km — it is just further to drive for the same thing. The useful
additions from doubling the radius are therefore the **closer** belts (40–110 km) filled
in properly, and **Falbygden** as a large‑field reserve.

### 5.2 Belts, ranked by usefulness

| # | Area | From Gothenburg | Character | Airspace / hazards | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Vårgårda – Herrljunga – Ljung – Vara** (Västgötaslätten) | ~65–110 km E (E20 / rv 42) | Large flat stubble fields, low tree lines, sparse buildings — the real open farmland closest to the city | Under Göteborg TMA (high floor); clear of any CTR; >10 km from any ATC field | **Best DIY option.** Pick a field, find the farmer. |
| 2 | **Varberg – Tvååker – Falkenberg** (Halland coastal plain) | ~65–110 km S (E6) | Open arable strip behind the coast, flatter and far less forested than Sjuhärad; steady sea breeze (watch drift) | Göteborg TMA; **Halmstad (ESMT) is controlled** — stay north/east of its zone; Ringhals nuclear plant has its own restricted area — stay well clear | **Strong second.** Shortest drive of the good options. |
| 3 | **Kungsbacka – Fjärås – Åsa – Frillesås** | ~35–55 km S | Coastal plain, stubble; nearest open farmland of all | Parts within/near **Landvetter's 10 km** → phone call needed; smaller field parcels | Usable but fiddlier than #1/#2; only for A–B motors on a calm day |
| 4 | **Falbygden — Falköping – Stenstorp – Skara** | ~110–140 km E/NE | The **largest open agricultural plateau in western Sweden** — big skies, long sightlines | East of the Såtenäs zone (see below); Ålleberg gliding traffic near Falköping; Skövde (ESGR) GA field | **Large‑field reserve.** Go here if belts #1–2 don't yield a landowner, or for D/E flights. |

### 5.3 Model‑flying fields in the wider radius

More SMFF/RC fields come into range (Slättlanda MFK at Vara, Lidköpings MFK, Vänersborgs
MFK at Brättelund, Trollhättans MFK, Falbygdens FK, Borås MFK, Kungsbacka MFK). All the
same caveats as §7: they are **RC‑circuit sized (120 m buffer typical)**, several sit
under the Såtenäs or Trollhättan‑Vänersborg (ESGT) zones, and **rocket motors are
normally not permitted** without specific club agreement. Treat as "call and ask", not
as sites. Full list + map: `faltregister.se`.

### 5.4 Skillingaryds skjutfält — historical only

Skillingaryd skjutfält (Vaggeryd, Småland, ~150 km ESE) was used for Swedish/Danish
amateur‑rocket launches decades ago (the "Pyro" group, with DARK). It is a **live
Försvarsmakten firing range** — access needs military coordination, that rocketry
arrangement is long dormant, and it is wildly oversized for a 235 g rocket on a B4.
Noted for completeness; **not a route to pursue** for the current vehicles.

### 5.5 Terrain reality

Whichever belt you choose, the field itself must still meet §6: open stubble/grass, clear
of forest edges, power lines, roads and water, big enough for the motor + drift. The
belts above are where such fields *exist* in quantity — they are not themselves the site.

## 6. Site specification (if arranging your own)

### 6.1 Minimum field size — NAR table + drift

NAR minimum site dimension = **shortest** rectangle side, or circle **diameter**:

| Motor | Total impulse | NAR min dimension |
|---|---|---|
| ¼A–½A | ≤ 1.25 N·s | 15 m |
| **A** | ≤ 2.5 N·s | **30 m** |
| **B** | ≤ 5 N·s | **60 m** |
| **C** | ≤ 10 N·s | **120 m** |
| **D** | ≤ 20 N·s | **150 m** |
| **E** | ≤ 40 N·s | **305 m** |

Then add **wind drift under canopy** on top:

- **Phoenix on C6** — apogee ~250–300 m, ⌀500 canopy at **4.8 m/s** ⇒ ~55–65 s hanging ⇒
  **~275–325 m downwind drift at 5 m/s wind.**
  → Want **≥ 400–500 m of clear ground downwind**, *or* hold a **wind limit of 3–4 m/s.**
- **Phoenix on A8 / B4** (the shakedown motors) — apogee 50–140 m ⇒ field need drops to
  **~100–200 m + drift**. A 3–4 m/s day on a mid‑size stubble field is enough.
- **SampleRocket 33‑36 on D/E** — **500–800 m clear radius**, or a strict wind cap. This
  tier wants Högsäter or the Västgötaslätten, not a Halland field.

Giving a launch day a **wind limit (≤ 3–4 m/s)** is more useful than chasing a bigger
field — descent time, not apogee, sets the drift.

### 6.2 Surface and surroundings

- **Mown grass, heath, or post‑harvest stubble.** September is the stubble window — no
  standing crop, farmers more amenable. No ripe/dry grass under the pad.
- Clear of **forest edges, power lines, roads, railways, livestock, buildings, open water.**
- Firm ground for the pad; rod/rail canted **5–10° into wind**, pointed away from people,
  roads and the nearest hazard, so the drift line runs back over open field.
- Spectators and cars **upwind** of the pad.

### 6.3 Paperwork for a DIY site

1. **Landowner consent — get it in writing** (email or text is fine). Agree access,
   parking, which field, and that you carry water/extinguisher and remove all debris.
2. **Fire‑ban check** that morning (§4).
3. If within 10 km of Landvetter or Säve: **call the airport** ahead, agree a window (§3).
4. Public land instead of private → add a **police permit** under ordningslagen 3:7.

## 8. Recommended path

1. **Join SMRK** (`smrk.space/medlemskap`); add Tripoli for the insurance.
2. Message the club (site form / FB group) to confirm the **next Högsäter launch day** and
   what's needed to fly there as a new member.
3. Bring **Phoenix on A8‑3 and B4‑4** for the first outings — clears `DESIGN.md` §11
   "field prep" without needing a big field, and Phoenix is not C‑ready anyway.
4. Only pursue a **Halland / Västgötaslätten farmland** site (§5, §6) if the ~1 h 20 drive
   to Högsäter is genuinely not workable — and then private land only, owner's OK, wind
   ≤ 3–4 m/s, fire‑ban checked, airport called if inside 10 km.
