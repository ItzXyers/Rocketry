SERVO POSITION  -- and why it is already as far forward as it can go
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Coaxial with an off-axis (X=-8) knob, the SG90's 22.7 mm body must lie on that
  line at the FORWARD face, case toward the tube centre (X -14.0 .. +8.8), Z 25.3..48.
  Clearances to the ejection hardware:
     servo case  -> knob D-flat         0.00 mm  (abutting; coupler enters the knob)
     servo case  -> cage nose           8.00 mm
     servo stack -> piston shaft, ARMED 6.95 mm   <-- the gap the user asked about
     servo stack -> piston shaft, FIRED 46.13 mm
  The 6.95 mm ahead of the servo is the KNOB (Z 48..60.5); the piston's retracted
  stub sits at Z 58 (POC Z-63 +121).  There is no usable pocket to move the servo
  further forward on this axis.  Reclaiming length needs a 2:1 crank/link from an
  off-axis servo to the knob crank pin (EJECTION_POC_DESIGN.md 10.3) -- a mechanism
  change, out of scope here.

  Servo torque path: the two SG90 mounting tabs (Comp_Servo_SG90_tab W/E) seat in
  cradle pockets -- shelf under + fore/aft walls -- gap to sled 0.00 = captured.
  They react the ~85 N.mm knob torque; without them the case would spin in the channel.

MODEL SIMPLIFICATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Comp_* are bounding solids; SG90 case boxed at 22.8x12x22.7 + two tab flanges.
  * SmallParts_Allowance = one 14 g block (regulator, microSD rdr, buzzer, C1, 2N7000,
    reed, SW1/SW2, wiring); the west crescent is spare volume for it too.
  * charger / BMP388 breakout / buzzer / C1 / switch dims ESTIMATED -- the 0.5 mm
    charger<->ADXL gap is within that uncertainty; measure before a printed sled.
  * Sled is a concept cradle+channel: tab pockets modeled, but no fastener bosses,
    no aft-closure register, no strain reliefs.
  * radial static port and the USB-C / microSD / buzzer wall cut-outs not modeled.
  * knob axis assumed to share the packing frame's +X; if the ejection module clocks
    the other way, mirror the servo to X = +8.
