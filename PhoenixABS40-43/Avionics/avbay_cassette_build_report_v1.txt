SUMMARY
  * recharge / SD / arm  : already external in the v2 pack -- no change, no disassembly
  * battery SWAP         : JST pigtail + bonded pull-loop, pocket open inboard -> spare
  * cassette removal     : split the bench joint, the fwd section lifts the whole
                          stack out -- servo/knob coupler never breaks
  * OPEN [R]             : the anchor bulkhead must be SCREWED not bonded (DESIGN.md
                          7.5), and that joint's recovery-snatch capacity is
                          UNVERIFIED until F_max (7.3) + a packed drop (7.9 t4)
  * TIGHT [E]            : the retention band tightens the sled's radial fit to
                          ~0.20 mm -- shorter FC board is the real fix
