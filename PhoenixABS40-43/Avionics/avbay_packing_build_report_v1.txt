MODEL SIMPLIFICATIONS  (read before taking any dimension downstream)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * every Comp_* body is a bounding box, not part geometry.  Real SG90 sides
    taper and its top is not a solid 22.7 slab; real PCBs are ~1.6 mm + parts.
  * Comp_SmallParts_Allowance is ONE 14 g block for the regulator, microSD
    reader, buzzer, C1, 2N7000, reed switch, SW1/SW2 and wiring -- which also
    fill interstitial gaps this block does not represent.
  * charger / BMP388 breakout / buzzer / C1 / switch dims are estimates.
  * radial static port (~Z12) and the USB-C / microSD / buzzer wall cut-outs
    are NOT modeled; FwdClosure is a plain disc.
  * Sled = 2 spine walls + mid rib + fwd bridge + 2 outboard lips.  No fastener
    bosses, no strain reliefs, no aft-closure register.
  * GPS, deploy MOSFET Q2, O-rings excluded per the study scope.
