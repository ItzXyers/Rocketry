UPDATE HISTORY (same session), oldest first: (1) grew the keel 50%, then
"widen more" -> 70deg -> 105deg -> 158deg total. (2) Servo re-mounted
axially, latch simplified to a bare hub+pin (no gate/arm/backstop) -- a
purely axial escape force produces zero torque about an axial shaft. (3)
BUG FOUND BY THE USER: axial servo port overshot the OD by 1mm, gouging
the skin -- fixed to OVERLAP=0.5mm margin + a hard assertion. (4) Added a
light ejection spring (Keel pocket + Door pusher tab), pushing the Door
axially forward. (5) v4.4: three linked changes -- (a) latch hub moved
fully INBOARD (was proud of the OD, a visible 16mm bump), (b) tip
ownership INVERTED from Door to Keel + keel widened again to 200deg
total, (c) spring reoriented to push the Door OPEN (tangential-at-the-
seam) with a captured-pin "battery door" mechanism: fixed pins/sockets at
the Door's fore (tip) end, servo-released keyhole latch at the aft end
only, plus a backstop (reintroduced -- the radial load DOES torque the
axial shaft, unlike the pure-axial case). (6) v4.5 (CURRENT, this
report): two follow-ups after the user reviewed a v4.4 render -- (a) "why
do we still have a giant rectangular hole" -- v4.4's hub fix only moved
the LATCH pin inboard, it never touched the SERVO BODY's own mount, which
had been flush with the OD (a ~26x18.5mm through-wall port) since (2)
above -- fixed by moving the servo's flange to the INNER wall instead, no
wall cut needed at all now. (b) "why do we have the latch arm still" --
a naming complaint, not a design one: the part had been called `LatchArm`
since before this session (a holdover from an older arm/gate/backstop
design); renamed to `ServoHorn` throughout. Numbers below reflect ALL SIX
updates. See NOSECONE_DESIGN.md for full rationale.

WHY V4.4/4.5: user feedback (2026-09-15, same session as v4/v4.2/v4.3):
"The bulk of the servo should be kept inside the rocket, only the tip
should point out (a 4mm D hole). Also, the nose point should be with the
keel not the door, and I want more of the rocket to be keel, less door.
Also nr 3; I want the spring to press against the thing from an 'upward'
perspective. I.e. it should try to open the door and the servo should
stop it. We should have a pin and socket mechanism that makes the door
only openable from the bottom (towards the ground when standing). Like
however they do some battery doors." Then, reviewing the v4.4 render:
"Where is the spring etc? And why do we still have a giant rectangular
hole?" and "why do we have the latch arm still?"

BUG CAUGHT THIS PASS, fixing the servo mount (not present in the shipped v4.4 build)

[BUG->FIXED] First attempt at moving the servo's mounting bosses inboard
       fused them at `SERVO_THETA +/- PAD_HOLE_THETA` with the old 20deg
       offset -- one boss landed outside the Keel's own angular span
       (KEEL_HALFW=100deg from centre), in territory already cut away for
       the Door, where the Keel has no material to fuse to. Came out as a
       disconnected floating solid (`spine` reported `solids=2`, not 1),
       caught immediately by inspecting the solid count rather than
       assuming the fuse worked. Fixed by shrinking `PAD_HOLE_THETA` to
       6deg (well inside the Keel's span) and adding a hard
       `len(spine.Solids) != 1` assertion so this can't silently recur.
