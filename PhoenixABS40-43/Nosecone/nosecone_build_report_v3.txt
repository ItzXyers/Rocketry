Generated by PhoenixABS40-43_Nosecone_v3.py, run via FreeCADCmd headless.
See NOSECONE_DESIGN.md for rationale. Supersedes nosecone_build_report_v2.txt
(v2 kept for history -- straight-seam lengthwise fairing split, since replaced).

WHY V3: user feedback on v2 (same session) -- (1) servo should point radially
out of the body, not just have a thin pad; (2) shock cord should thread
through the door and click into place, not just anchor at the bulkhead;
(3) the door should be an oval cut, bigger than a "middle" split, covering
more of the nose. V3 keeps v2's hinge+latch principle but replaces the
straight-seam split with a true oval (ellipse) boundary, sizes the servo
mount to the real measured SG90 envelope, and adds printed snap-in shock
cord clips on the door.

DESIGN DECISIONS MADE THIS REVISION (flagged for user review, not silently assumed)

1. Hinged doors cannot exceed 180 deg of arc -- a door subtending more than
   that contains two diametrically-opposite points, making its own width
   (2R) exceed the mouth it must pass through at every opening angle: it is
   geometrically trapped, not just hard to hinge. Built to 170 deg (peak),
   as close to that ceiling as GAP_DEG leaves room for. "More than middle"
   is realised as more of the nose's LENGTH (Z 35-178, most of the forward
   nosecone) rather than more arc. Be clear this is a trade: v3's door is
   actually SMALLER than v2's by every measure (19.8g vs 31.2g, tapers to
   points instead of running full width the whole 182mm length) -- v2's
   straight 180/180 split was already the largest a hinged door can be.
   The oval SHAPE was the explicit ask; if maximum open area matters more
   than the oval shape, that's a different trade to make deliberately, not
   something this revision assumed.
2. Servo shaft stays radial (matches the request) but mounted THROUGH the
   wall rather than fully external: the tab flange sits at the outer
   surface, only the short shaft-side of the case (~5mm) is proud outside,
   the long base-side (~17.7mm) hangs in the already-open bay cavity. A
   fully-external mount would have put ~17-23mm of servo case sticking
   straight out of a 43mm-OD tube -- a large, aerodynamically bad blister.
   This is an ASSUMPTION about which side of the SG90's mounting flange is
   closer to the shaft -- flagged above, bench-verify.
3. Shock cord's PRIMARY anchor stays the M4 bulkhead eyebolt (unsplit
   Spine, unchanged from v2) -- this still carries the actual parachute
   snatch load. The door's new snap clips are routing/dressing features
   only (un-proven cantilever geometry), not a load path. If flight loads
   are meant to run through the door instead, that is a bigger, separate
   change (the hinge + latch would need to be sized for snatch load, not
   just holding the door shut) -- flag if that's actually what's wanted.
