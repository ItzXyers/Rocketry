[4] the stop -- where the rocket hangs, and what the lug sits on
  StopPost top Z 280.5 = lug aft end (station 187.5)
  cap aft face Z 111.0 -> 50 mm above the plate top (nozzle standoff)
  lug + web end face 30.3 mm2, of which 16.7 mm2 lands on the post (55 %)
  rocket 274 g = 2.69 N -> 0.16 MPa bearing; x5 for a dropped load: 0.80 MPa
  (the groove side opens toward the body -- the web's root and the lug's
   body-side arc overhang the U; that is the cap-rib clearance, see [6])
  PASS -- lug lands on solid post top, stress trivial

[5] lean on the lug slop -- where does the cap end up?
  lug bore d4.4 on d4.0 over 35 mm -> up to 0.65 deg of lean
  the CG is 26 mm off the rod, so the rocket DOES lean: its foot swings toward
  the rod by up to 1.9 mm at the cap -- more than the 1.0 mm rib-to-rod gap.
  So it leans until the cap ribs touch the ROD (normal force ~0.41 N, friction
  ~0.12 N along the rod -- negligible).  The post is cut back to CRADLE_R 24.25 so
  the cap touches the rod, never the post: checked in [6] at full lean.
  NOTE for the airframe: this is a property of the D9aft lug, not of the pad.

[6] LOADING -- the rocket slides down the rod past the post
  cap ribs (r 22.75) swept Z 111..281, upright:          0.000 mm3
  same, leaning 1.0 mm onto the rod (worst, [5]):        0.000 mm3
  radial clearance cap ribs -> cradle, upright: 1.50 mm
  fins swept from rest up past the post top:             0.000 mm3
  PASS -- the rocket loads past the post, even leaning

[7] ROTATION KEY -- swing the rocket about the rod until it hits the post
  at rest (0 deg): rocket vs post 0.000 mm3, vs plate/hub 0.000 mm3
   +6.5 deg: first contact is the cap against the post
   -6.5 deg: first contact is the cap against the post
  -> rocket keyed to +-6.5 deg; nozzle wanders <= 2.9 mm (cap hole r 7.5)
  PASS -- keyed by cap/body on the horns; fins never touch the post

[8] FUSE CLIP
  clip: d2.2 bore (Klima fuse d2.0), snap-in throat 1.5 opening outward, 14 mm tall
  clip 14 mm off the nozzle axis; bore TILTED 18.0 deg so the fuse, pushed straight
  up through it, crosses the cap face 1.6 mm off the nozzle axis (opening r 7.5)
  and is 5 mm inside the cap on the axis.  Clip top Z 80, cap face Z 111.
  with the +-6.5 deg key the fuse still lands 2.9 mm inside the opening edge
  nearest clip/arm material to the nozzle axis: 10.0 mm (jet core r 7.5).
  It is ABS in the plume's edge for the ~0.2 s before the rocket clears:
  expect soot and some surface melt -- inspect each flight, reprint if soft.
  PASS -- fuse reaches the nozzle, clip outside the jet core

[9] blast plate coverage
  nozzle at r 25.95 from the rod -> 51.5 mm of plate on the near-edge side,
  at ANY rotation (plate is round and centred on the rod).  Jet lands on alu,
  50 mm below the nozzle, beyond the d44 column (plate overhang there 3.9 mm).
  No vent holes: the plate is open all round, gas leaves sideways.
  PASS -- > 40 mm of plate round the nozzle

[10] GUIDED ROD LENGTH (v1 [6] convention: lug FULLY off the rod)
  lug Z 280.5..315.5 at rest
  rod   1000 mm -> guided    684 mm   light >=1000 SHORT   payload >=1200 SHORT
  rod   1300 mm -> guided    984 mm   light >=1000 SHORT   payload >=1200 SHORT
  rod   1400 mm -> guided   1084 mm   light >=1000 ok   payload >=1200 SHORT
  rod   1500 mm -> guided   1184 mm   light >=1000 ok   payload >=1200 SHORT
  BUY >= 1316 mm of rod (light) / >= 1516 mm (payload).
  OPEN -- the 274 g D9aft with its e-bay is closer to the PAYLOAD line than the
  light one.  Set the .ork rail length to the guided figure for the rod you buy
  and read rail-exit speed (>= 15 m/s, DESIGN 4.4) before calling it.

SUMMARY
  * Print: StopPost v2 (new), PadHub v1 STL, 3 x PadLeg_v2.stl (v1 leg, pose fixed,
    0.6 mm print web closing the tenon slot -- CUT IT before assembly).
  * Buy: alu disc d155 x 2.0, drill ONE d20.5 centre hole (step bit).
  * Assemble: legs into hub, plate over the spigot, StopPost over the ROD's
    bottom end, rod down the hub bore to the ground, post sitting on the spigot.
  * Load: turn the post so the fuse arm points the way the body will hang, slide
    the rocket down until the lug sits on the post top (Z 280).  Snap the fuse
    sideways into the clip's throat and slide it up the tilted bore, through the
    cap opening, into the motor core.
  * Rocket keyed to +-6.5 deg; nozzle 50 mm above the plate.
  * Rod: >= 1316 mm (light) / >= 1516 mm (payload rule) -- OPEN until the .ork
    gives rail-exit speed at that length ([10]).
  * FAIL [11]: bare pad tips at 8.5 m/s -- peg the feet / ballast the hub.
  * Fuse: Klima d2.0, 80-110 s/m -> cut 375 mm for ~30 s to walk away
    (30 s at the fast end), plus what goes into the motor and through the clip.
  * ASSUMED: plate 2.0 mm.  UNPRINTED and UNFLOWN.
  RESULT v2: FAIL 11 (bare pad, deliberate: stake it); OPEN 10
