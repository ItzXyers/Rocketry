This is every electrical part on the sled v8 e-bay and how it connects. The bay is a logging altimeter and fires nothing, because D9aft deploys on the motor charge. The reed switch only drives the gate of a P-FET, and the FET carries the load. The battery charges off the vehicle through its JST-PH lead. Numbered markers point to six open items, listed below the drawing, to resolve before the first power-up in the airframe.

Open items before first power-up

  1. 1

    Power does not stay on once the magnet comes off

    SW1 is normally open and BOM.md lists the magnet as a ground tool that does not fly. Wired as drawn, Q3 conducts only while the magnet sits on the ARM mark. Remove it and R6 pulls the gate back to BATT+, so the board shuts off. No document in the repo describes a latch.

    Simplest fix: use a normally-closed reed. The magnet then becomes the remove-before-flight safe (on = off, removed = powered), and nothing else in the drawing changes. Alternative: a soft latch, where firmware holds Q3's gate low through a small N-FET on a free pin (D4 or D5) and the magnet only starts the board. That costs one FET, one resistor and a firmware change.

  2. 2

    Buzzer type must match the firmware

    buzzer.cpp holds D9 high or low with no tone generation, so it needs an active buzzer that oscillates on its own. The sled v7/v8 notes describe the module as "KY-006-style", and a KY-006 is usually a passive transducer. Driven with DC it only clicks.

    Check: put 5 V across the module's + and − pins. A steady tone means it is active and fine. A single click means it is passive. In that case, swap in an active module or change buzzer.cpp to drive a 2–4 kHz LEDC tone on D9. Confirm the module pinout too: some KY boards leave the middle pin unconnected.

  3. 3

    R6 may already be on the reed module

    KY-021 boards normally carry a 10 kΩ resistor between + and S. With the module wired + → BATT+, S → Q3 gate, − → GND, that resistor already does R6's job.

    Check with a meter before wiring. If the 10 kΩ is between + and S, leave R6 off. Armed drain is then about 0.4 mA through the 10 kΩ, which is negligible against 620 mAh.

  4. 4

    VIN is 1 V under the Nano's spec

    Carried over as BOM item O1. U2's fixed 5 V feeds VIN, which is rated 6–21 V. Community testing says it runs, but there is no margin. C1 is fitted in sled v8, which covers the brownout case the August notes required before using the Wi-Fi interface near a loaded vehicle.

    If the board resets on SD writes or Wi-Fi bursts, suspect this before the sensor wiring.

  5. 5

    SW2 has no place on sled v8

    The sealed bay has no wall hole for a switch, and sled v8 places no SW2. Without it, R3 holds D7 low, so the Wi-Fi AP never starts. That is the safe default, but it means no pad checkout over Wi-Fi.

    Options: a 2-pin header with a jumper set before the bay closes (the AP is then up whenever the board is powered, until confirmed BOOST), or a second reed on its own ARM mark wired the same way, 3V3 → reed → D7 with R3 to GND.

  6. 6

    D6 is still driven at apogee

    The firmware still pulses PIN_DEPLOY (D6) at apogee. On this vehicle nothing is connected to it: no Q2, no coil. That is harmless as long as it stays that way.

    Leave D6 unconnected. Don't reuse it for the SW1 soft latch or anything else until the deployment code is compiled out for this vehicle.

What changed since the August diagram

Removed

  • BQ25302 USB-C charger (U3). Sled v8 deleted it and its wall slot. Charge at J1, off the vehicle.
  • GPS and its Serial1 link. D2/D3 stay reserved.
  • Servo and burn-wire channels. D9aft deploys on the motor charge.

Added or now drawn

  • SW1 reed arming through the wall, switching Q3 + R6 as a high-side load switch (BOM item O2).
  • SW2 + R3 interlock on D7, which config.h marked "not yet in the schematic".
  • Qwiic wired as a star at the Nano, not a daisy chain.
  • Airframe interfaces: static port to U4, sound port to BZ1, ARM mark over SW1.
